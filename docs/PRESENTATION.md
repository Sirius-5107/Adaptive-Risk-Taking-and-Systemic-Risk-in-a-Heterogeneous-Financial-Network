# Presentation Plan

Target: 8–10 research-style slides.

1. Research question
2. Motivation: interconnected institutions and contagion
3. Model: heterogeneous network + risky/conservative strategies
4. One deterministic simulation
5. Monte Carlo systemic-risk estimation
6. Connectivity/shock experiment
7. Adaptive risk-taking mechanism
8. Adaptive results
9. Validation and limitations
10. Extensions / research questions

## Presentation rule
Do not put an equation on a slide unless every symbol can be explained verbally.

The presentation should demonstrate:
- mathematical formulation
- statistical reasoning
- simulation design
- validation
- interpretation
- awareness of limitations

Avoid presenting it as a finished real-world banking model.
