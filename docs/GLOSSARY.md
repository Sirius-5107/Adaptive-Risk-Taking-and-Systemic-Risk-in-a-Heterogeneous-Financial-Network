# Glossary

**Financial network** — Institutions represented as nodes with financial obligations represented as directed edges.

**Exposure** — Amount one institution is financially exposed to another.

**Shock** — An adverse external change affecting assets or returns.

**Contagion** — A loss at one institution reduces payments to another, potentially causing further losses.

**Clearing** — Determining how much each institution can actually pay after accounting for assets, liabilities, and incoming payments.

**Payment fraction** — The fraction of an obligation that is ultimately paid.

**Fixed point** — A set of payment values that reproduce themselves when the clearing rule is applied.

**Monte Carlo** — Repeating a randomized experiment many times to estimate probabilities and averages.

**Systemic risk** — Risk that failures spread across enough of the network to produce a system-level event.

**Heterogeneity** — Institutions differ in strategy, risk exposure, or network connections.

**Adaptive behaviour** — Strategy choices change in response to observed outcomes.

**Replicator dynamics** — A rule where strategies with higher payoff tend to become more common.

**Statistical uncertainty** — Variation caused by finite random simulation samples.

**Model uncertainty** — Uncertainty about whether the chosen model assumptions represent the phenomenon adequately.

**Robustness** — Whether conclusions persist when reasonable assumptions, seeds, or parameters change.
